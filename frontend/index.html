1. Model Overview & Architecture

The production system runs an ensemble Gradient Boosting Regressor with 150 estimators, standard scaling, and one-hot encoding for categorical crop commodities.

3. Assumptions & Limitations

  • Assumes regular mandi market trading without sudden statutory price freezes or unexpected cross-border trade bans.
  • Calibrated specifically for Wheat, Rice (Paddy), and Potato regional markets.
  • Extreme 100-year catastrophic flooding or unseasonal climate anomalies may deviate outside typical ±1.0 MAE bands.

4. Ethical Safeguards

Forecasts provide confidence bounds to prevent false precision and protect smallholder farmers from distress selling. Final selling agreements remain with cooperative members.
